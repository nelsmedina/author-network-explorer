A.N.E

Author Network Explorer

One-time setup

A.N.E reads publication data from OpenAlex, a free and open catalogue of scholarly work. Since February 2026 OpenAlex requires every request to carry an API key, so you'll need your own.

It's free, takes about 30 seconds, and includes a daily usage budget that is far more than normal browsing needs.

Your OpenAlex API key

  1. Create a free OpenAlex account and copy your key.
  2. Paste it here and save.

Where your key is stored

Your key is saved in Chrome's own extension storage on your computer and synced to your other Chrome profiles if you have Chrome sync switched on. It is sent only to api.openalex.org, and never to us or to anyone else. Nothing about your key is stored in the extension's files.

If your key ever leaks, rotate it from your OpenAlex settings — that invalidates the old one immediately — then paste the new one here.
